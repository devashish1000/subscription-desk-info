Subscription Desk terms of use

This installation is a personal tool for its account owner. It organizes receipt evidence, drafts requests and tracks merchant responses.

The owner must review the exact sender, recipient, subject, body and requested amount before approving any outbound request. Editing a draft clears its approval. Sending an email does not establish that a subscription has been cancelled or that money has been refunded.

Receipt evidence may be incomplete. Estimated renewal dates, uncertain subscriptions, refund promises and bank settlement must remain distinct. Merchant decisions, Google access and service availability can change.

The dashboard runs locally and monitors mail while its backend and computer are running. The standard functionality does not require paid AI. Contact the owner at devashishn10@gmail.com.
