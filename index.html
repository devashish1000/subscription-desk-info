PERSONAL SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT

Subscription Desk

A personal dashboard for subscription receipts, recurring charges, renewal dates, cancellation requests and refunds.

The dashboard runs on its owner's computer. Gmail access helps find billing evidence and track merchant replies. Every outbound email requires the owner to review and approve the exact draft.

This informational website has no sign-in, mailbox connection, financial records or subscription data. The actual application and its records remain on the owner's computer.
