Subscription Desk privacy notice

Subscription Desk is a personal application operated by its account owner on their own computer.

The app accesses Gmail to find subscription receipts, renewals, cancellation replies and refund evidence. Sending is available only for the exact email draft the owner reviews and approves.

Subscription records, mailbox evidence, drafts and action history are stored locally. OAuth credentials are stored in an encrypted local credential store and are excluded from ordinary JSON exports. A financial backup may contain email evidence; the owner controls its storage and deletion.

Google receives the requests needed for Gmail authentication and API access. The standard dashboard does not require an OpenAI API key. Optional AI integrations are separate, require explicit configuration, and may send selected evidence to their provider. Paid AI is currently disabled in this installation. If the owner requests assistance through Codex or connected tools, the information shared in that task is handled under that service's privacy terms.

Gmail data is used for the owner's subscription management, is not sold or used for advertising, and is not shared with merchants except in a message the owner explicitly approves. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local records remain until the owner deletes them. Google access can be revoked at Google Account permissions. The owner can stop the app and remove its local runtime data to delete stored evidence and credentials.
